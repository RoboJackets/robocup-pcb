1. BEFORE ORDERING
------------------
1.1 Powerboard agreement on J15: +5V (pin 2) sits next to +3V3 (pin 3); a GND pin between
    them, pin 7/8 UART order, 3V3 setpoint 3.4V +/-3% and never above 3.6V. There is no
    overvoltage protection on the board, so a 5V-on-3V3 crimp fault kills the MCU (M1, M2).
1.2 Parts (JLCPCB assembly, stock checked 2026-11-03; DESIGN_NOTES.md §14.1 has every
    substitution). Upload ControlBoard2027_BOM_jlcpcb.csv (161 placements, LCSC part numbers)
    with the CPL from the jlcpcb/ plugin. ControlBoard2027_BOM_full.csv lists every line with
    its source and the Digi-Key/Mouser alternate. Not stocked at JLCPCB, so order through
    JLCPCB global sourcing or consign: D7-D11, J9, J13, U7 (ControlBoard2027_BOM_digikey.csv)
    and U11 (ControlBoard2027_BOM_mouser.csv). SH1-SH6 (shunts) and SK1 (J13 socket) are loose
    LCSC parts fitted by hand. TP1-TP21 are excluded. Before ordering:
    - Re-check stock in the JLCPCB BOM tool (it changes daily). Tightest on 2026-11-03: U2
      STM32H723ZGT6 195, J16/J18 TSW-103 399, shunts 928.
    - Check the substitutes JLCPCB shows in the placement preview: F1/F3/F4 Littelfuse
      1812L075/24DR, F5/F6 1812L125/16DR, SW1/SW2/SW4-SW6 C&K PTS636SM43LFS, SW3 Hroparts
      DS-06BP, X1 ABM8-25.000MHZ-10-D1G-T, J3/J14/J17 generic gold headers.
    - U11 changed from LSM6DSK320X to LSM6DSV320X (same pins and ranges): update the IMU driver
      (WHO_AM_I, register map) and confirm vibration behaviour on the robot.
    - Check the Adafruit 326 interface jumpers are set to I2C; keep J16 on MUX when powered
      from USB (VBUS capacitance).
    Order U10 as SN74AHCT125DR (SOIC-14), not the obsolete D tube option.
1.3 J1 is now a Hirose DM3AT-SF-PEJM5 push-push socket (KiCad library footprint).
1.4 J3 (SWD) is an unkeyed 1x5 2.54mm header: match the pin-1 marker.
    J9 is a Samtec LSHM-110-02.5 mezzanine (same part as RadioBoard2027 J2); the radio plugs
    straight on and hangs off the east edge. Check retention on the first assembly (M6).
1.5 Rail budget: +5V total must stay under F5's 0.95A hold at 50C. Confirm the powerboard can
    source >2.5A per rail into a fault so F5/F6 actually trip (M3).
1.6 OLED: order the STEMMA QT variant (M5).
1.7 Kicker interface: J10 is signals only (1 GND, 2 SCL, 3 SDA); the kicker is powered from the
    powerboard. Confirm this pin order against the kicker design (M10). The radio link J9 is
    matched to RadioBoard2027 J2; change both projects together if either moves.
1.8 ERC reports one error: the U2 VCAP pin 71/106 power-output tie (m21). It is intended;
    exclude it in KiCad ERC.

4. LAYOUT STATUS
----------------
Routed on 4 layers (JLC04161H-7628: L1 signal, In1 GND, In2 +3V3, B.Cu low-speed + GND).
DRC: 0 errors, 0 unconnected, schematic parity clean; two silk-over-copper warnings remain.
- USB D+/D-: 90R differential (0.30/0.20mm) over In1 GND.
- SDMMC CLK/CMD/DAT0-3: 50R, all 44.45-45.15mm including R6; GND return vias at the D2/D3
  layer changes.
- ESP SPI (J9): SCK 52.00, MOSI 52.25, MISO 51.82, CS 51.74mm (vias 1.6mm each, resistor spans
  counted, no stubs), R65/R66 (both 0402) at the MCU, 45° trapezoid length bumps. CS crosses the
  others in a 4.4mm B.Cu hop over an In2 GND patch (priority above the +3V3 plane); nothing routes
  between R65/R66 pads; R29/R31/R32 sit on their lines. With the radio board, SCK/MOSI/CS match
  within 0.17mm end to end.
- Known deviations: J9 (0.5mm-pitch mezzanine under the radio) has no per-pin silkscreen labels;
  the radio lines converge to the 0.5mm pad pitch in the last 1-2mm before J9 (under 3W there),
  and HANDSHAKE runs its last 2.6mm at 0.2mm (DESIGN_NOTES.md §2).
- Known deviations: MISO/CS widen 0.25 -> 0.36mm about 5mm from the MCU, not at a pad (since
  the first layout); J8 pin labels sit just above J12 (DESIGN_NOTES.md §2).
- Silkscreen: pin/signal labels on every connector, button and the DIP (DESIGN_NOTES.md §2).
- HSE: X1, C18, C19, R12 beside PH0/PH1.
- Red ROUTING notes on each sheet stay the authoritative rules for any rework.

6. MAINTENANCE
--------------
Close KiCad before scripted schematic edits.
Commit or stash before any history rewrite (git filter-repo resets the working tree).
After each edit: geometry check, text overlap check, netlist diff by net membership, ERC.
Schematic conventions:
  - Grid 1.27mm.
  - Power symbols (#PWR) need project-unique refs.
  - Deleting a power symbol can remove a junction a mid-wire pin still needs; re-check
    the netlist after any delete.
  - Global labels named +5V/GND/+3V3 join the power nets (used on J4-J8).
  - +3V3_PB is only used on sheet 01 (U9 VIN1). The radio link (07) and LEDs (06) use the mux
    outputs +3V3 / +5V.
