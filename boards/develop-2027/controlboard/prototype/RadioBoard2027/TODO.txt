1. BEFORE ORDERING
------------------
1.1 Antenna: decide on the 5GHz gain issue (C1) and confirm the MHF I plug (m1).
1.2 Parts (JLCPCB assembly, stock checked 2026-11-03; DESIGN_NOTES.md §6 BOM has every
    substitution). Upload RadioBoard2027_BOM_jlcpcb.csv (35 placements) with the CPL from the
    jlcpcb/ plugin. Not stocked at JLCPCB, so order through JLCPCB global sourcing, consign or
    fit by hand: U1 (RadioBoard2027_BOM_mouser.csv), U4, J2 and ANT1
    (RadioBoard2027_BOM_digikey.csv). RadioBoard2027_BOM_full.csv lists every line with its
    source and the Digi-Key/Mouser alternate. C4 is DNP and TP1/TP2 are excluded. Re-check
    stock before ordering.
1.3 Check STM32H723 pin tolerance for PB4-PB7 against a USB-powered radio with the
    controlboard off (M2).
1.4 Layout: placed and routed, 45° bends. DRC: 0 errors, 0 warnings, 0 unconnected, schematic parity
    clean; footprints match their libraries (DESIGN_NOTES.md §7, including "Known deviations").
    Open in eeschema and save each sheet once: KiCad then drops the unused embedded symbol copies
    (stock TPD4E05U06DQA and the superseded TPD4E05U06DQA_FT) that the scripted edits left behind.
1.5 J2 is a Samtec LSHM mezzanine (same part as ControlBoard2027 J9, 5mm stack); the radio
    hangs off the controlboard on J2 alone. Check retention on the first assembly (M4).
1.6 Board Setup > Net Classes: Default clearance must stay 0.15mm (set in the project file while
    KiCad was closed). If it shows 0.2mm after reopening, set it again in the dialog.
1.7 J2 and D4 are the only bottom-side parts: order bottom assembly for them or hand-solder them
    (J2's shield pins are paste-in-hole).
1.8 Clean up the older off-pad T-joins and width steps listed in DESIGN_NOTES.md §7 "Corners".


2. FIRMWARE
-----------
ESP-Hosted co-processor: SPI mode 3, CLK=GPIO6, MOSI=GPIO7, MISO=GPIO2, CS=GPIO10,
HANDSHAKE=GPIO3, DATA_READY=GPIO4, reset=EN (M1). Checksum on. Bring up at 5-10MHz.
Keep MISO/HS/DR Hi-Z while PWR_SRC_HOST (GPIO9) is low (M2). Status LED on GPIO24.
Host: ignore HANDSHAKE/DATA_READY for 500ms after releasing EN (DATA_READY floats near 2.28V
during ESP reset, DESIGN_NOTES.md §5).
First board: module supply at C1 during 5GHz TX (m3), USB enumeration (m5).


3. LAYOUT CONSTRAINTS
---------------------
Red ROUTING notes on each sheet are the authoritative routing rules. As routed: see
DESIGN_NOTES.md §7. In short:
- C1/C2 at module pad 2; R1/C3 short to EN; R4 at GPIO2; R5/R6 at GPIO13/14.
- USB: J1 -> U2 -> R5/R6 -> module in one direction, no vias. Full-Speed only; the pair is not
  edge-coupled (U2 pinout), see DESIGN_NOTES.md §7.
- J2 on B.Cu; one via per line in its row gap, then U5/U6 flow-through on F.Cu above it; SPI on
  B.Cu over the In2 GND region, MOSI/CS matched to SCK over both boards (0.17mm).
- Wide traces for +3V3_HOST and +3V3 (650mA peaks). U4 tab to a copper pour.
- Module EPAD to GND with vias.
- Custom rules in RadioBoard2027.kicad_dru use the hierarchical net names (e.g.
  '*/ESP_SPI_*', '/ESP32-C5/USB_DP'); update them if sheets or labels are renamed.


4. MAINTENANCE
--------------
Close KiCad before scripted schematic edits. Signals cross sheets through hierarchical labels and
root-sheet pins; rails (+3V3_HOST, GND) use global labels and power symbols.
Power symbols (#PWR) are numbered by sheet (1xx..4xx).
Keep J2 pinout identical to ControlBoard2027 J9; change both together.
