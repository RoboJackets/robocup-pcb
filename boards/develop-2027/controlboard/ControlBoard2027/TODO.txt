bookkeeping - NEITHER can produce a wrong board, because power symbols are
virtual and never reach the netlist (verified: 0 of 151 netlist components are

0.1 DUPLICATE #PWR REFERENCES  (28 references duplicated across sheets)
    Every sheet independently numbers its power symbols from #PWR001, so
    #PWR001..#PWR028 each appear on several sheets. Cause: konnect's
    add_power_symbol picks "the lowest number free ON THE SHEET", not
    project-wide. 144 power symbols total.

0.2 STALE INSTANCE PATHS  (50 of them)
    05_IMU (15), 08_DIP (14), 09_Buttons (21) still carry symbol instance
    entries pointing at sheet UUID 625025f8-a6e0-4c52-95c6-7a2fc84d0410 - the
    old combined "IMU, DIP and Buttons" sheet, deleted when it was split three
    ways. Cause: duplicate_sheet copies the source's instance data and
    delete_sheet does not prune the orphans.
    `kicad-cli sch upgrade --force` does NOT clear these (tested).

    FIX FOR BOTH, one action:
      Open the project in Eeschema -> Tools -> Annotate Schematic
        - scope: entire schematic
        - "Keep existing annotations" (so R/C/U references are NOT disturbed;
          they are referenced by name throughout PART_AUDIT.txt and the sheet
          notes)
      then File -> Save All.
    Annotating reassigns unique #PWR references and rewrites instance data for
    the live hierarchy, dropping the orphans.
    VERIFY AFTERWARDS: re-run ERC, and diff the netlist by membership - it must
    come out IDENTICAL, since none of this is supposed to touch connectivity.

3. BUSES ON THE ROOT SHEET - NOT DONE, AND WHY
================================================================================
The review asked for "real KiCad buses everywhere". A first attempt put buses on
the root with a local label on each sheet-pin stub. Eeschema rejected it: ~200
ERC errors, all label-dangling. Root-sheet local labels beside sheet pins do not
resolve. That attempt was removed.

The CORRECT KiCad pattern is bus-type sheet pins: each child sheet carries ONE
hierarchical label named {SIG_A SIG_B ...}, fans it out to individual signals
INSIDE the child, and the root then draws bare bus wires between bus pins - no
labels on the root at all. Labels work fine on child sheets.

Cost: the fan-out (bus + bus entry + label per signal) moves into all nine child
sheets. konnect has no batch tool for labels or bus entries, so it is ~200
individual calls. Deferred pending a decision that it is worth it.

Current root is direct pin-to-pin: crossing-free, ERC-clean, ports grouped by
protocol - which addresses the "not a spaghetti" half of the comment.

4. ELECTRICAL FINDINGS STILL OPEN
================================================================================
4.1 SUSTAINED 3V3 OVERVOLTAGE IS NOT PROTECTED  (accepted limitation)
    D10 (SMAJ4.0CA) does not conduct until 4.44 V; STM32 VDD abs max is 4.0 V.
    Transient/ESD only. No flat-clamp part exists below 5 V Vrwm. A powerboard
    regulator failure putting 5 V on the 3V3 rail kills the MCU.
    Upgrade path: active OVP load switch (TPS2595 / NCP360) in place of F3/Q2.
    The 5 V rail IS protected - D9 TVS0500 is a 6.0 V flat clamp chosen against
    the TPS2116's 6.0 V abs max.

4.2 USB-ONLY OPERATION IS BENCH PROGRAMMING ONLY  (documented on sheet 01)
    U3 TLV1117-33 is 800 mA and dissipates 1.4 W at that. The 3V3 budget is
    ~1000 mA (150 mA STM32 + 650 mA radio + 200 mA SD). Run from the powerboard
    for full-system operation.
    Related: F1 is a 500 mA PTC whose hold current derates to ~375 mA at 50 C.
    Consistent with bench-only use; if bench draw approaches 400 mA go to 1.1 A.

4.3 I2C CABLE LENGTH
    Kicker/expansion pull-ups are now 2.2k. t_r ~ 0.85*R*C: 2.2k into 200 pF is
    ~370 ns against the 300 ns Fast Mode limit - still marginal. If those cables
    run long, drop to 1.5k and re-check.

4.4 5V TOLERANCE ON THE EXPANSION/KICKER I2C PINS
    Those headers supply +5V while their pull-ups go to +3V3. Fine for
    open-drain, but if an attached board pulls up to 5 V that lands on
    PF14/PF15/PB10/PB11. Confirm those are FT pins before shipping cables.

4.5 MINOR
    - SW4/SW5/SW6 Value fields say USER_BTN0/1/2; doc specifies PTS820 - put the
      MPN in the BOM fields.
    - One benign wire crossing remains on 03_USB_Debug (no junction, not a
      short). Cosmetic.
    - No LSE / RTC crystal: PC14-OSC32_IN is USER_EXTI2. PC15 is free if one is
      ever needed - move USER_EXTI2 to PE1 (pin 142, EXTI1 unused).

6. HOW THIS SCHEMATIC IS MAINTAINED
================================================================================
All schematic edits go through the konnect MCP server - do not hand-edit the
.kicad_sch files. KiCad must be CLOSED while konnect writes, or it refuses with
a file-lock error.

Two checkers live in the session scratchpad and should be re-run after any edit:
  checksch.py  - copper geometry: wire crossings (flagging any that carry a
                 junction, i.e. a real short), collinear overlaps, wires through
                 symbol bodies, symbol-on-symbol overlap, pins sitting mid-wire
                 with no junction, duplicate junctions.
  checktext.py - readability, driven off kicad-cli's SVG text metrics: label on
                 label, notes inside chip bodies, text outside the page frame.

Every change is verified by diffing the generated netlist against the previous
one by NET MEMBERSHIP, not by text. ERC does not catch shorts created by
junctions that konnect auto-inserts at wire crossings. That check has caught:
two real shorts, a VBUS scope regression, a sheet-split that silently
cross-connected the IMU/DIP/button nets, and an orphaned VDDA cap row.

Known konnect quirks to watch for:
  - Wire coordinates are SNAPPED to the 1.27 mm grid. Placing a sheet at x=400
    and a wire at x=400 leaves a 0.05 mm gap, because the wire lands on 400.05.
  - It auto-inserts junctions at T-intersections, so adding your own on top
    produces duplicates. checksch.py catches these.
  - add_power_symbol numbers #PWR references per sheet, not per project.
  - Wires it writes have no (stroke ...) block. Harmless - KiCad defaults it.
