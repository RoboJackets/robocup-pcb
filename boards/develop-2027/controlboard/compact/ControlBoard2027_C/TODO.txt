1. BEFORE ORDERING
------------------
1.1 Re-audit the last changed areas with an independent reviewer (CLAUDE.md section 12): the back-edge connector
    change (J10 / U17, J15, R33 / R34, the J3 move, power-entry copper) and the audit-2 / audit-3 fixes (added GND
    vias, moved TP2 / TP6 / R27 / R69, sheet 02 boxes). Then refill zones, save, and re-run DRC with parity and ERC.
1.2 C1: confirm the BB-2020BGR-TRB pad drawing is a top view (vendor or sample); mirror the footprint if not.
1.3 Confirm the 50 ohm (0.15 mm L1 / L6, 0.12 mm L4) and 90 ohm (0.14 / 0.15 mm) widths in JLC's impedance
    calculator for JLC06161H-3313E; order with impedance control.
1.5 Powerboard agreement on J15 (JST PH 7P): 1 TX (out of this board, PA0 via R33), 2 RX (into this board,
    PA1 via R34), 3 GND, 4 +3V3,
    5 GND, 6 +5V, 7 GND. 3V3 setpoint 3.4 V +/-3 %, never above 3.6 V. One 2 A contact per rail; keep the steady load
    per rail well under 2 A. The powerboard must source > 2.5 A per rail into a fault so F5 / F6 trip (M1, M2, M3,
    deviation 17).
1.6 Kicker agreement on J10 (JST GH 4P: 1 SDA, 2 GND, 3 SCL, 4 GND, signals only) (M10).
1.7 Motor-module agreement on J4-J8 (JST GH 6P: 1 MOSI, 2 GND, 3 MISO, 4 SCK, 5 GND, 6 CS); the module board's J2
    is still PH 8P (M19). Modules must release MISO while CS is high (M9).
1.8 OLED module (M5): choose the 0.96in SSD1306 I2C module; check its pin order (1 GND, 2 VCC, 3 SCL, 4 SDA), address,
    power-on reset, pull-ups and mounting-hole positions against the J13 footprint.
1.9 Parts: the BOM is in bom/ (2026-11-07); generate the placement file. Re-check stock in the
    JLCPCB BOM tool; check the substitutes in the placement preview (F1 / F3 / F4 1812L075/24DR, F5 / F6
    1812L125/16DR, SW1 / SW2 / SW4-SW6 PTS636SM43LFS, SW3 DS-06BP, X1 ABM8-25.000MHZ-10-D1G-T). Source D7-D11 and U11
    outside JLCPCB stock (global sourcing or consign). Re-check J9 (C3643542, 37 on 2026-11-07 = 18 board sets
    with the radio J2) and U7 (MCP1826S-3302E/DB, C638624) the day of ordering. Order U10 as SN74AHCT125DR (SOIC-14). Order with tented
    vias (deviation 23) and double-sided assembly.
1.10 Any change to J9, HR1 or HR2 must be made in this board and both radio projects together (INTERFACE.md).

3. FIRMWARE (see ../docs/FIRMWARE.md)
-------------------------------------
3.1 Apply the clock tree (HSE 25 MHz, C2), VOS0 or HPRE /2 (M16), BOR level (M7), PWR_LDO_SUPPLY.
3.2 Re-pin the .ioc from the netlist pin map (M11): SPI6 LEDs, SPI2 right wheels, SPI4 dribbler, SDMMC2, SPI3 radio.
3.3 PB6 = ESP_DATA_READY, PB7 = ESP_HANDSHAKE (swapped 2026-11-07): swap the pin defines and EXTI6 / EXTI7.
3.4 SPI1 / SPI2 SCK, MOSI and the motor CS pins at OSPEEDR LOW or MEDIUM, never HIGH / VERY_HIGH (deviation 11).
3.5 SPI3 radio: CRC off, 8-bit (M4); bring up at 3 MHz, run at 24 MHz; CS PG15 at OSPEEDR LOW / MEDIUM.
3.6 Master Keep IO State on all SPI masters (M17).
3.7 IMU U11 (bottom side, rotation 270, pin 1 north-west seen from above): derive the axis remap from the datasheet
    axis drawing, mirrored for the bottom side; use the LSM6DSV320X driver (WHO_AM_I, register map); SW_RESET after
    brown-out (m16).
3.8 Power-source changes (poll PA2 / PA3, m11): re-initialise SD, IMU and OLED, pulse ESP_RST and restart
    ESP-Hosted, resend the LED frame. LED current cap about 250 mA, about 100 mA while PWR_SRC_5V is low.
3.9 SD: no card-detect pin (report "present", treat init failure as "no card"), CMD0 recovery (M18).
3.10 I2C bus recovery before each init; OLED probe 0x3C / 0x3D; let CubeMX recompute I2C timing.
